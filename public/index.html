Our Purpose

Many students lack early access to hands-on engineering experiences, leaving them unaware of their potential in STEM. This gap limits creative exploration and problem-solving at a critical age.

Youth Engineers Initiative exists to build creative confidence and fill the gap in early STEM education through engaging, hands-on workshops that inspire curiosity and empower the next generation of innovators.

Workshops

Hands-on learning in collaborative spaces where students build and experiment.

Mentorship

Connecting students with knowledgeable engineers and industry mentors.

Applying engineering skills in real-world problem-solving contexts.

Start Your Own Chapter

If you are a high school student and are interested in starting a Youth Engineers Initiative School Chapter, follow the instructions below:

Making a School Chapter Guide

Step 1 — Find Your YEI Team: Recruit at least 3–5 passionate students who want to lead your chapter. Designate roles: President, VP, Secretary, and Outreach Lead.

Step 2 — Get School Approval: Meet with your school's club coordinator or principal. Submit a club charter or application form. Get a faculty advisor to sponsor your chapter.

Step 3 — Register with YEI: Complete the official YEI Chapter Registration Form. Submit a chapter plan including meeting schedule, goals, and planned activities.

Step 4 — Plan Your First Meeting: Announce your chapter on social media and school bulletin boards. Organize a launch event, workshop, or info session. Connect with other chapter leaders across the network.

Step 5 — Run Your Chapter: Hold regular meetings and workshops. Complete at least one community project per semester. Submit reports to YEI National and maintain your chapter status.

Why Start a Chapter?

Leadership Experience

By starting a Youth Engineers Initiative School Chapter, students gain leadership experience in areas such as after-school programs, organizing meetings, and running a chapter.

Community Network

Existing Chapters provide students a platform to showcase engineering projects, earn recognition, and connect with peers across regions.

National Support

Join the University of Members — Ambitious students starting a chapter will gain access to an exclusive community of fellow Youth Engineers Initiative chapter leaders across the nation.
